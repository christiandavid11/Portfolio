About Me

I’m a Computer Engineering graduate with a background in software development, technical support, and incident management.

As a Software Engineer, I developed and maintained C# applications while working with SQL databases, REST APIs, and Azure. In client support, I’ve strengthened my experience in troubleshooting, root cause analysis, authentication issues, and technical documentation.

My interest in cybersecurity grew through the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate, where I gained foundational knowledge and hands-on experience in security operations, threat detection, network security, and incident response.

Through the program, I gained introductory practical exposure to security tools such as Splunk and Google Security Operations (Chronicle) for SIEM and log analysis, Suricata for intrusion detection, and Wireshark and tcpdump for network traffic analysis.

I'm now taking a deeper dive into Security Operations Center (SOC) operations, SIEM investigations, emerging cyber threats, attack techniques, vulnerability research, and defensive security practices. I regularly explore resources such as CISA security advisories, the CVE database, and KrebsOnSecurity to stay informed about evolving threats and vulnerabilities.

Beyond my foundational training, I'm committed to strengthening my practical skills through further hands-on exercises, security investigations, and independent exploration of cybersecurity tools and techniques.

I'm currently preparing for the Microsoft SC-900 certification, followed by CompTIA Security+, as part of my continued development toward a career in cybersecurity.

Beyond technology, I enjoy photography and poetry. Photography allows me to explore creativity and appreciate details from different perspectives, while poetry gives me a way to express thoughts and ideas through writing. I also enjoy playing guitar in my spare time.
